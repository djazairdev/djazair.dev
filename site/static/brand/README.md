# Brand assets

## GitHub logo

`github-invertocat-white.svg` is the unmodified white Invertocat from GitHub’s official logo bundle, downloaded on 8 October 2026:

- Logo assets and usage: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo
- Original bundle: https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip

GitHub’s logo is a trademark of GitHub, Inc. It is used as a social link to the djazairdev organization. The renderer preserves its original path, white fill and viewBox proportions when inlining the mark.

## Android robot

`android-head-flat.svg` is Google's official flat robot artwork, retrieved on
19 October 2026 from the SVG link in its brand guidelines:

- Source: https://developer.android.com/static/images/brand/android-head_flat.svg
- Usage and attribution: https://developer.android.com/distribute/marketing-tools/brand-guidelines
- Licence: CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The asset retains its original geometry and colours. The renderer adds a viewBox,
decorative accessibility attributes and size, and gives each clip ID a unique
prefix so repeated uses do not duplicate an HTML ID. The teacher page's
footer carries Google's required robot credit and Android trademark attribution.
The logo is not covered by this site's MIT or CC BY 4.0 licences. Trademark rights
remain with Google; publication still needs the applicable brand review.

The iOS coming-soon control uses a generic phone outline from the site's own
stroke-icon system, not an Apple logo or App Store badge. It is not a store link.
